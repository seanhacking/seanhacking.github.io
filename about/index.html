About

Sean Hacking

Mechanical engineer. I take hardware from a rough sketch to a tested prototype to production.

I'm a mechanical engineer who gets hardware from a napkin sketch to a flight-tested prototype to production, quickly.

Most recently I was on Zipline's New Products team, where I took the second-generation drone-loading kiosk through 9 flight-tested prototypes across 3 generations. Before that I founded Veer, a cycling drivetrain company, where I invented the products, set up in-house CNC production and worked with our manufacturing vendors. After Veer I set up Form Robotics, a prototyping shop built around five CNC machines.

I like owning whole systems: the mechanism, the test rig that proves it, and the cost and supplier work that gets it shipped. I'm a named inventor on 6+ patents, granted or pending. When something keeps failing, I find out why and fix the design.

I studied bioengineering at UC Santa Cruz and started out building liquid-handling robots for synthetic DNA manufacturing.

Working together

I take on mechanical engineering projects: a prototype from sketch to tested hardware, a DFM and cost-down push, a test rig, or a mechanism that keeps failing. The contact page has the ways to reach me.
