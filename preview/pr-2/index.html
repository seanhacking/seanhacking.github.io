How I work

Sketch. Prototype. Test. Production.

I like owning whole systems: the mechanism, the test rig that proves it, and the cost and supplier work that gets it shipped.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    A rough sketch, then CAD. 15+ years in SolidWorks, from motion studies to large assemblies.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Same-day CAD to part. 12+ years programming and running CNC: 3–5 axis mills and live-tooling lathes.

  3. 03

    Test

    Custom test rigs, accelerated life testing, flight testing, and root-cause analysis when something fails.

  4. 04

    Production

    Design for manufacture: injection moulding, die casting, sheet metal, extrusion, and suppliers from prototype to 10K+ units.

The story so far

From DNA robots to drone kiosks

Seven drawings, one belt. Scroll and it runs.

  1. 01BuildCambrian Genomics, 2014–2015

    Robots that handle DNA

    I studied bioengineering at UC Santa Cruz, then built and programmed liquid-handling robots for synthetic DNA manufacturing. I also invented the first process for recovering monoclonal DNA clusters from Illumina flow cells.

  2. 02InventVeer, 2016

    A belt that opens and closes

    I founded Veer to make belt drivetrains for bikes. I invented Split Belt Pro and the V-Link detachable belt splice, which created the first belt-drive conversion category for cycling and won the Eurobike Startup Award, competing against Gates.

    The project: A belt that opens and closes →

  3. 03TestVeer

    The rig that found the failure

    Our early belts failed. In one week I built a drivetrain test suite that goes beyond ISO standards, including a universal testing machine made from a log splitter and a load cell. It found the root cause, and the redesigned belt lasts 20,000 miles.

    The project: The rig that found the failure →

  4. 04PatentVeer

    Shifting under load

    SHIFT is a seamless belt transmission that shifts under load, without a clutch. I invented it, wrote and prosecuted the patent myself to grant in the US and internationally with no rejections, and designed it for manufacture.

    The project: Shifting under load →

  5. 05AutomateVeer

    Ten minutes down to thirty seconds

    Putting a belt together took about 10 minutes. I designed and programmed LinkBot, an automated belt-assembly tool that does it in under 30 seconds, with fewer than 1 fault per 4,000 rivets.

    The project: Ten minutes down to thirty seconds →

  6. 06PrototypeForm Robotics, 2024–2025

    A shop for same-day parts

    I built a prototyping shop around five CNC machines, set up for same-day design-to-testable-part, and invented the LavaLock universal workholding system: 91% less setup time and 50% lower prototype cost. We machined next-day prototypes for aerospace and robotics customers, including Zipline.

    The project: A shop for same-day parts →

  7. 07FlyZipline, 2025–2026

    Nine prototypes, all flown

    On Zipline's New Products team I took the second-generation Dropbox, a robotic drone-loading and package-pickup kiosk, from rough sketches through 9 flight-tested prototypes across 3 generations. The results: more than 10x improvement in key package-pickup reliability metrics, and about 3x lower cost than the current production generation.

    The project: Nine prototypes, all flown →
