At Veer our early belts failed. I needed a universal testing machine, so I built one from a log splitter and a load cell. The whole drivetrain test suite cost about $500 and took a week, and it tests beyond the ISO standards.

With it we found the root cause of the failures and redesigned the belt to a 20,000-mile lifespan.
