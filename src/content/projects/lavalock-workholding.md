At Form Robotics I built a prototyping facility with 5 CNC machines from scratch, set up for same-day design-to-testable-part. LavaLock came out of that shop.

## LavaLock

- A universal workholding system: **91% reduction in setup time** and **50% savings on prototype cost**.
- Alongside it I developed a custom thermoplastic resin: **561 PSI against 300 PSI** for a commercial resin.

## The shop

We machined next-day prototypes for aerospace and robotics customers, including motor housings and bulkheads for Zipline. MVP and early sales came within 6 months.
