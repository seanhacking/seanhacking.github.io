Veer was my hardware company. We built belt drivetrains for bikes, from zero to $1.4M in revenue, with a team of 8, in-house CNC production and 4 key manufacturing vendors.

## The product

I invented and commercialised **Split Belt Pro** and the **V-Link** detachable belt splice. Together they created the first belt-drive conversion category for cycling.

- Shipped **10,000+ drivetrain units** to 16 OEMs, 3 international distributors and 43 dealers.
- Created 70+ pulley designs and an accessory catalogue for drop-in fitment across millions of vehicles.
- Won the **Eurobike Startup Award**, competing against Gates.

## Testing on a shoestring

Our early belts failed. I built a drivetrain test suite that exceeds ISO standards for **$500 in one week**, including a universal testing machine made from a log splitter and a load cell. With it I found the root cause and redesigned the belt to a **20,000-mile lifespan**.

## LinkBot

Belt installation took about 10 minutes. I designed and programmed **LinkBot**, an automated belt-assembly tool that does it in **under 30 seconds** with fewer than 1 fault per 4,000 rivets. It was delivered to 3 OEMs on a $10K, four-month budget.
