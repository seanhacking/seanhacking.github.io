The Dropbox is Zipline's robotic drone-loading and package-pickup kiosk. I worked on the second generation on the New Products team.

## What I did

- Started from rough sketches and took the design through **9 flight-tested prototypes across 3 generations**.
- Was the sole R&D engineer through the early architecture, then owned every system that interacts with the active package (the Droid and Pod) on the design team.
- Invented and developed several of the systems that defined the kiosk architecture.
- Ran the prototypes through lab and flight testing on tight cycles, and worked with electrical, software, NPI and supply chain toward production.

## Results

- **More than 10x improvement** in key package-pickup reliability metrics.
- **About 3x lower cost** than the current production generation, through architecture decisions and design for manufacture.
- Named inventor on at least one patent application.
