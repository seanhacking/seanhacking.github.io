Rules

  • Safety yellow is a field colour, never a text colour. Text on it is always graphite.
  • Hazard stripes mark one thing per page at most: the results band edge or the CTA.
  • Headlines are Big Shoulders, uppercase, tight. Body text is never condensed.
  • Labels are engraved tags: Martian Mono uppercase in a thin bordered plate, square corners.
  • No rounded corners, no shadows. Hover = a 4 px yellow bar under the element.
